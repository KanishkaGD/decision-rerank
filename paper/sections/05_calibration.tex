\section{Are Decision Models Better Calibrated?}
\label{sec:calibration}

\input{tables/calibration}

A model is well calibrated if its probabilities match how often passages are actually relevant. We report two
measures (Table~\ref{tab:calibration}). The expected calibration error (ECE) measures only this match. The Brier
score, the mean squared error of the probabilities, also rewards a model that separates relevant from non-relevant
passages well. Calibration is the main selling point of decision models, and the base models are indeed poorly
calibrated (ECE up to 0.750). Decision fine-tuning improves both measures for every model, but so does reranker
fine-tuning. Compared with the reranker fine-tune of the same model, the decision fine-tune has a higher Brier score
at 0.6B, 0.8B, 4B and 8B (e.g.\ Kev-4B 0.207 vs.\ Prism-4B 0.172) and a higher or similar ECE (Kev-4B 0.216 vs.\
0.218). Only at 9B is Clef-Flash better than Prism (ECE 0.140 vs.\ 0.242), and there Ours-9B has the lowest ECE of
the study (0.111, below every decision model including the 27B ones and \jev{}) and a Brier score as low as the
best decision model (0.157). Good calibration thus comes from a loss that treats the score as a probability, not from decision
fine-tuning.

\paragraph{Uses of calibration.}
The same picture holds for practical uses of the probabilities (results in our repository). If every passage with
$p \ge 0.5$ is kept, e.g.\ to decide which passages reach a generator, the best reranker fine-tune selects the better
set for every model where both exist, and a fixed threshold works across collections for Prism and bge as well as
for the decision models. Summing a query's probabilities estimates its number of relevant passages about equally
well for decision and reranker fine-tunes, and best for Ours-9B. Calibrating a reranker after training with
temperature or Platt scaling on MS~MARCO dev does not reach the decision models, because MS~MARCO judges only about
one passage per query. A well calibrated model is better, but it need not be a decision model, as Ours-9B shows.
