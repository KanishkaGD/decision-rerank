\paragraph{Reranker fine-tuning wins on every backbone.}
On all five backbones where both exist, the reranker fine-tune ranks better than the decision fine-tune in either
mode (Table~\ref{tab:triplets}; average over ten collections, Qwen3-0.6B: 56.1 vs.\ 27.0; Qwen3-8B: 59.4 vs.\ 52.7;
Qwen3.5-0.8B: 57.1 vs.\ 36.0; Qwen3.5-4B: 58.7 vs.\ 51.3; Qwen3.5-9B: 59.0 for Prism and 59.4 for Ours-9B vs.\ 54.7
for Clef-Flash), and on almost every single collection. The zero-shot subset tells the same story (e.g.\ 54.9 vs.\
30.9 at 0.6B, 56.1 vs.\ 49.0 at 9B), so the gap is not owed to in-domain MS~MARCO training; nor to proprietary data,
since Ours-9B, trained on MS~MARCO alone, matches Prism-9B. Decision fine-tuning barely moves the zero-shot base
model -- its better mode lies between 3.5 points below (Kev-9B) and 3.7 above (Kev-27B) the base average -- whereas
reranker fine-tuning adds 5 to 32 points. We ran no significance tests; the gaps are several points on every
backbone. Only on the backbone without a reranker fine-tune does a decision fine-tune lead: Kev-27B (joint) is the
best system of the study (61.1), but only edges reranker fine-tunes a third its size (59.4).

\paragraph{Joint vs.\ isolated.}
Joint scoring helps only models trained on long states: Kev-27B gains 1.3 points and \jev{} 1.0
(Table~\ref{tab:context-short}), Kev-9B and Clef stay within 0.3, Kev-4B and Clef-Flash lose about 1.5 to 2, and
Kev-0.6B, Kev-0.8B and Kev-8B lose 10.7 to 13.9. According to their cards~\cite{KevCards}, Kev-0.8B and Kev-4B were
trained on states of at most 7,552 tokens and Kev-27B on up to 32,768, whereas a joint state has about 13k tokens on
TREC DL and 40k or more on BEIR. Beyond the trained length a question is no longer tied to its passage: Kev-0.6B
joint separates relevant from non-relevant passages at chance level on DL 2019/2020, and its probabilities rise with
a passage's position in the state. (Kev-8B's card states no training length.)

\paragraph{Context.}
The open Kev-27B (61.1) matches the closed \jev{} (60.5, Table~\ref{tab:context-short}); both lead the established
rerankers (Set-Encoder-large 59.0, monoT5-3B 57.5, RankZephyr-7B 57.3), as do Qwen3-Reranker-8B and Ours-9B (59.4)
at a third of Kev-27B's size. \laya{} (44.0) is barely above BM25 (43.2).
