\paragraph{Reranker fine-tuning wins on every backbone.}
On all five backbones where both exist, the reranker fine-tune ranks better than the decision fine-tune in either
mode (Table~\ref{tab:triplets}; average over ten collections, Qwen3-0.6B: 56.1 vs.\ 27.0; Qwen3-8B: 59.4 vs.\ 52.7;
Qwen3.5-0.8B: 57.1 vs.\ 36.0; Qwen3.5-4B: 58.7 vs.\ 51.3; at 9B: 59.0 for Prism and 59.4 for Ours-9B vs.\ 54.7
for Clef-Flash). The lead is significant on 46 of the 60 backbone--collection pairs and never significantly
reversed ($^\ast$). The zero-shot subset tells the same story (e.g.\ 54.9 vs.\ 30.9 at 0.6B, 56.1 vs.\ 49.0 at 9B),
so the gap is not owed to in-domain MS~MARCO training; nor to proprietary data, since Ours-9B, trained on MS~MARCO
alone, matches Prism-9B. Decision fine-tuning barely moves the zero-shot base model up to 9B -- its better mode lies
between 3.5 points below and 3.2 above the base average, with significant differences in both directions on at
most five collections ($^\dagger$) -- whereas reranker fine-tuning adds 5 to 32 points. Only Kev-27B improves on its
base (by 3.7, significant on eight collections); on that backbone, without a reranker fine-tune, it is the best
system of the study (61.1), but only edges reranker fine-tunes a third its size (59.4).

\paragraph{Joint vs.\ isolated.}
Joint scoring helps only models trained on long states, and only slightly: Kev-27B gains 1.3 points and \jev{} 1.0
(Table~\ref{tab:context-short}; Kev-27B significantly on one collection), Kev-9B and Clef stay within 0.3, Kev-4B and
Clef-Flash lose about 1.5 to 2, and Kev-0.6B, Kev-0.8B and Kev-8B lose 10.7 to 13.9, significantly on 6 to 10
collections ($^\ddagger$). Kev-0.8B and Kev-4B were trained on states of at most 7,552 tokens, Kev-27B on up to
32,768~\cite{KevCards} (Kev-8B's card states none), whereas a joint state has about 13k tokens on TREC DL and 40k or
more on BEIR. Beyond that length a question is no longer tied to its passage: Kev-0.6B joint separates relevant from
non-relevant passages at chance level on DL 2019/2020, its probabilities rising with the position in the state.

\paragraph{Context.}
The open Kev-27B (61.1) matches the closed \jev{} (60.5, Table~\ref{tab:context-short}); both lead established
rerankers (Set-Encoder-large 59.0, monoT5-3B 57.5), as do Qwen3-Reranker-8B and Ours-9B (59.4). \laya{} (44.0) is
barely above BM25 (43.2).
