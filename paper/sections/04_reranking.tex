\section{Decision vs.\ Reranker Fine-Tuning}
\label{sec:reranking}



\paragraph{Reranker fine-tuning wins on every model.}
For all five foundation models where both exist, the reranker fine-tune ranks better than the decision fine-tune in
either mode (Table~\ref{tab:triplets}; average over ten collections, Qwen3-0.6B: 56.1 vs.\ 27.0; Qwen3-8B: 59.4 vs.\
52.7; Qwen3.5-0.8B: 57.1 vs.\ 36.0; Qwen3.5-4B: 58.7 vs.\ 51.3; Qwen3.5-9B: 59.0 for Prism and 59.4 for Ours-9B vs.\
54.7 for Clef-Flash). The difference is significant on 46 of the 60 pairs of model and collection, and the decision
fine-tune is never significantly better ($^\ast$). The zero-shot subset shows the same (e.g.\ 54.9 vs.\ 30.9 at 0.6B,
56.1 vs.\ 49.0 at 9B), so the gap does not come from training on MS~MARCO. It does not come from special training
data either, as Ours-9B, trained on MS~MARCO alone, matches Prism-9B. Up to 9B, decision fine-tuning hardly changes
the base model: its better mode lies between 3.5 points below and 3.2 points above the base average, with
significant differences on at most five collections ($^\dagger$). Reranker fine-tuning adds 5 to 32 points. Only
Kev-27B clearly improves on its base model (by 3.7 points, significant on eight collections). It is the best system
in our study (61.1), but only slightly ahead of reranker fine-tunes a third of its size (59.4).

\paragraph{Joint vs.\ isolated.}
Scoring all candidates together helps only models trained on long states, and only a little: Kev-27B gains 1.3
points and \jev{} 1.0 (Table~\ref{tab:context-short}). Kev-9B and Clef stay within 0.3, Kev-4B and Clef-Flash lose
1.5 to 2, and Kev-0.6B, Kev-0.8B and Kev-8B lose 10.7 to 13.9 points, significantly on 6 to 10 collections
($^\ddagger$). Kev-0.8B and Kev-4B were trained on states of at most 7,552 tokens and Kev-27B on up to
32,768~\cite{KevCards} (Kev-8B's card gives no length), while a joint state has about 13k tokens on TREC DL and 40k
or more on BEIR. Beyond the trained length the model loses track of which passage a question is about: Kev-0.6B in
joint mode separates relevant from non-relevant passages only at chance level on DL 2019/2020.
