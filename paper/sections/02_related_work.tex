\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related-work}

\paragraph{Fine-tuned rerankers.}
Cross-encoders fine-tuned on MS~MARCO~\cite{Nogueira2019,Nogueira2020monoT5} and their LLM successors read relevance
from the logit of a relevance token or a scoring head; recent pointwise LLM rerankers fine-tune open backbones such
as LLaMA~\cite{Ma2023RankLLaMA}, Qwen3 (Qwen3-Reranker~\cite{Zhang2025Qwen3Embedding}) and Qwen3.5
(Prism~\cite{Zhang2026Prism}). Listwise rerankers output a ranking instead~\cite{Sun2023RankGPT,Pradeep2023RankZephyr};
the Set-Encoder lets passages attend to each other while staying permutation-invariant~\cite{Schlatt2025SetEncoder}.
Abdallah et al.~\cite{Abdallah2025HowGood} compare many of them on the BM25 candidates we use.

\paragraph{System One models in retrieval.}
Decision models are trained to answer questions about a state with a probability (decision fine-tuning,
RLCD \FLAG{cite}); open fine-tunes exist as Clef~\cite{Clef} and Kev~\cite{Kev,KevCards}, and \laya{} is another
open System One model~\cite{Laya}. Their use as rerankers has so far been studied only in non-peer-reviewed reports: S1Rank and
jev-rerank score BM25 candidates with \jev{}~\cite{S1Rank,JevRerank}, blog posts report on \jev{} as a
reranker~\cite{LanceDB2026,Elastic2026,Hindsight2026}, and \laya{} has been used as a relevance gate before
recall~\cite{Smriti2026}. None compares decision fine-tuning with reranker fine-tuning on the same backbone.

\paragraph{Calibration and cutoffs.}
Modern neural networks tend to be overconfident, and temperature scaling is a simple post-hoc
fix~\cite{Guo2017Calibration}; neural rankers are no exception~\cite{Penha2021Calibration}. Calibrated relevance
probabilities matter wherever a score must be read on an absolute scale, e.g.\ to truncate a ranked list before it
is passed on~\cite{Bahri2020Choppy}. We test whether decision models offer such probabilities where reranker
fine-tunes do not.
