\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related-work}

\paragraph{Fine-tuned rerankers.}
Cross-encoders fine-tuned on MS~MARCO~\cite{Nogueira2019,Nogueira2020monoT5} and their LLM successors read relevance
from the score of a relevance token or a scoring head. LLM rerankers fine-tune open models such as
LLaMA~\cite{Ma2023RankLLaMA} or Qwen~\cite{Zhang2025Qwen3Embedding,Zhang2026Prism}. Listwise rerankers output a ranking instead~\cite{Sun2023RankGPT,Pradeep2023RankZephyr},
and the Set-Encoder lets passages attend to each other~\cite{Schlatt2025SetEncoder}. Abdallah
et al.~\cite{Abdallah2025HowGood} compare many of them on our candidates.

\paragraph{System One models in retrieval.}
Besides \jev{}~\cite{TypeSafe2026Jev}, Kev~\cite{Kev}, Clef~\cite{Clef} and \laya{}~\cite{Laya} are open
System One models. Their use as rerankers
has so far only been tried in reports that are not peer-reviewed: S1Rank and jev-rerank score BM25 candidates with
\jev{}~\cite{S1Rank,JevRerank}, blog posts report on \jev{} as a
reranker~\cite{LanceDB2026,Elastic2026,Hindsight2026}, and \laya{} has been used as a relevance gate before
recall~\cite{Smriti2026}. None compares decision fine-tuning with reranker fine-tuning on the same model.

\paragraph{Calibration and cutoffs.}
Modern neural networks tend to be overconfident, and temperature scaling is a simple fix after
training~\cite{Guo2017Calibration}; neural rankers are no exception~\cite{Penha2021Calibration}. Calibrated
probabilities matter wherever a score is read on an absolute scale, e.g.\ to cut off a ranked
list~\cite{Bahri2020Choppy}.
