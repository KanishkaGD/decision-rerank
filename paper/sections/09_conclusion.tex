\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

System One models are not better rerankers. On every backbone where both exist, plain reranker fine-tuning beats
decision fine-tuning, by 5 to 29 points nDCG@10 from 0.6B to 9B, and up to 9B decision fine-tuning adds nothing over
simply asking the base model. Only at 27B, where no reranker fine-tune exists, does a decision model stand out, and it
is barely ahead of reranker fine-tunes a third of its size. The calibration that decision models are advertised for
comes from the question, not from the model: a question that matches the relevance labels makes decision and base
models alike much better calibrated, a looser one takes the advantage away, and either way calibration does not buy
better passage selection. If you need a reranker, fine-tune a reranker.
