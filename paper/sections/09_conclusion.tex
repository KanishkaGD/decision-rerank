\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

System One models are not better rerankers. On the same backbone, decision fine-tuning never makes the better
reranker: plain reranker fine-tuning wins by 5 to 29 points nDCG@10 from 0.6B to 9B, and the two tie at 27B. Up to 9B,
decision fine-tuning adds nothing over simply asking the base model. The calibration that decision models are
advertised for comes from the question, not from the model: a question that matches the relevance labels makes
decision and base models alike much better calibrated, a looser one takes the advantage away, and either way calibration
does not buy better passage selection up to 9B. If you need a reranker, fine-tune a reranker.
