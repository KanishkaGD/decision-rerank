\paragraph{Collections.}
TREC DL 2019--2023~\cite{Craswell2020DL19,Craswell2021DL20} on the MS~MARCO v1 and v2 passage
corpora~\cite{Nguyen2016MSMARCO}, and TREC-COVID, NFCorpus, SciFact, Touch\'e and DBPedia from
BEIR~\cite{Thakur2021BEIR}. TREC-COVID, Touch\'e and DBPedia have no training split in any of the
fine-tunes' data and form the zero-shot subset.

\paragraph{Candidates.}
Every reranker sees the BM25 top-100 of Pyserini~\cite{Lin2021Pyserini} ($k_1=0.9$, $b=0.4$; for
DL 2021--2023 the official top-100 files, which are the same set) -- the candidates of
S1Rank~\cite{S1Rank}, RankGPT~\cite{Sun2023RankGPT}, RankZephyr~\cite{Pradeep2023RankZephyr} and
the comparison of Abdallah et al.~\cite{Abdallah2025HowGood}, whose numbers we report where we do
not recompute them (marked in the tables).

\paragraph{Decision models and modes.}
All decision models are asked S1Rank's yes/no question~\cite{S1Rank}, fixed in advance, and score
each candidate either jointly (all candidates of a query in one state) or in isolation (one
candidate per request); both are pointwise. \TODO{Context systems: \jev{}, \laya{} (isolated only),

nDCG@10 with ir\_measures~\cite{MacAvaney2022irmeasures}; paired $t$-tests over queries,
