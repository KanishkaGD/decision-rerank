\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\paragraph{Triplets.}
On each of six backbones (Qwen3-0.6B/8B, Qwen3.5-0.8B/4B/9B, Qwen3.8-27B) we compare three objectives.
\emph{None}: the post-trained release, asked the decision models' question in its chat format (thinking off),
scored by $P(\text{yes})$ at the first answer token. \emph{Decision fine-tuning}: Kev~\cite{Kev,KevCards} (LoRA on
the -Base checkpoints up to 9B, a full fine-tune of the post-trained 27B), Clef-Flash (9B) and Clef
(27B)~\cite{Clef}. \emph{Reranker fine-tuning}: Qwen3-Reranker~\cite{Zhang2025Qwen3Embedding} (on the -Base
checkpoints Kev starts from) and Prism~\cite{Zhang2026Prism}, scored by $P(\text{yes})$ over the ``yes''/``no''
logits; none is public for 27B. On Qwen3.5-9B we add our own (\emph{Ours-9B}), trained without decision data: LoRA
($r=32$) on the post-trained release, one epoch over 100k MS~MARCO training queries with one positive and 15 hard
negatives each, InfoNCE plus binary cross-entropy on the yes/no logit, training queries matching any test topic
removed, checkpoint chosen on 200 MS~MARCO dev queries. Qwen3-Reranker, Prism and Ours-9B saw MS~MARCO; Kev's
sources contain neither MS~MARCO nor BEIR~\cite{KevCards}.

\paragraph{Collections and candidates.}
TREC DL 2019--2023~\cite{Craswell2020DL19,Craswell2021DL20,Craswell2021DL21,Craswell2022DL22,Craswell2023DL23} (MS~MARCO v1/v2 passages~\cite{Nguyen2016MSMARCO}) and
TREC-COVID, NFCorpus, SciFact, Touch\'e and DBPedia from BEIR~\cite{Thakur2021BEIR}; TREC-COVID, Touch\'e and
DBPedia have no training split in any fine-tune's data and form the \emph{zero-shot subset} (ZS). Every system
reranks Pyserini's BM25 top-100~\cite{Lin2021Pyserini} (official top-100 files for DL 2021--2023, the same set),
the candidates of S1Rank~\cite{S1Rank}, RankGPT~\cite{Sun2023RankGPT}, RankZephyr~\cite{Pradeep2023RankZephyr} and
Abdallah et al.~\cite{Abdallah2025HowGood}, whose published numbers we use where we did not recompute them.

\paragraph{Decision models.}
All decision models get S1Rank's question~\cite{S1Rank}, fixed in advance (``Is document \texttt{\{doc\}} relevant
to the \texttt{query}? Relevant means it answers the query or contains information that directly addresses it.''),
with a state holding all 100 candidates (\emph{joint}) or one (\emph{isolated}); both modes are pointwise. Besides
the triplets we run \jev{} 1.13.0 (closed), \laya{}~\cite{Laya} (isolated only; its context is too short),
monoT5-3B~\cite{Nogueira2020monoT5}, bge-reranker-v2-m3~\cite{Chen2024BGEM3}, the
Set-Encoder~\cite{Schlatt2025SetEncoder} and RankZephyr (DL only).

\paragraph{Metrics.}
nDCG@10 (ir\_measures~\cite{MacAvaney2022irmeasures}); expected calibration error (ECE, 15
bins)~\cite{Guo2017Calibration} on the judged pairs of DL 2019--2023 (relevant from grade 2), the only collections
with judged non-relevant passages. \emph{One cutoff}: keep every candidate with $p \ge 0.5$; set $F_1$ and its regret
against the best cutoff per collection (unjudged = non-relevant). \emph{After the fact}: temperature and Platt
scaling of each reranker's logit, fitted on the BM25 top-100 of 1,000 MS~MARCO dev queries. \emph{Sanity checks}:
our BM25 matches Pyserini's reproductions~\cite{Pyserini2CR}, monoT5-3B and RankZephyr are within 0.2 points of
their published DL 2019/2020 numbers~\cite{Abdallah2025HowGood}, \jev{} joint within 0.5 of S1Rank's~\cite{S1Rank}
on four of five shared collections (1.5 below on TREC-COVID).
