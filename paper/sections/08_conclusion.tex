\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

System One models are not better rerankers. On the same backbone, decision fine-tuning never makes the better
reranker: plain reranker fine-tuning wins by 5 to 29 points nDCG@10 from 0.6B to 9B, and the two tie at 27B. Up to 9B,
decision fine-tuning adds nothing over simply asking the untuned model. The calibration advantage of decision models
depends on the question. A question that matches the relevance labels makes decision and untuned models alike much
better calibrated; with a looser one, decision models lose their advantage over reranker fine-tunes. Up to 9B,
calibration also does not make up for a worse ranking when passages are kept at a fixed threshold. If you need a
reranker, fine-tune a reranker.
