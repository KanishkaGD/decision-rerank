\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Do System One models make better rerankers? No. On the same foundation model, reranker fine-tuning beats decision
fine-tuning, which adds little to the untuned model. Their calibration depends on the wording of the question, does
not make them select better passages, and is matched by our reranker fine-tune. The untuned 27B model counts
relevant passages best.
Our second question was not chosen on development data, and calibration was only measured on TREC DL.
