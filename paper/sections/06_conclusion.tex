\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Do System One models make better rerankers? No. On the same foundation model, reranker fine-tuning beats decision
fine-tuning, which adds little to the untuned model. Their calibration is real only with a question that matches the
relevance definition, does not make them select better passages, and can be matched by a reranker fine-tune.
Our second question was not chosen on development data, and calibration was only measured on TREC DL.
