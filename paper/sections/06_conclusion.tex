\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

On the same backbone, System One models do not make better rerankers: reranker fine-tuning beats decision
fine-tuning wherever both exist, also without in-domain training data (RQ1). Nor are decision models at least better
calibrated: reranker fine-tunes are about as well calibrated and give the better set at a fixed cutoff, and one
trained with a pointwise loss on its yes/no logit beats every decision model (RQ2). The probabilities that set
decision models apart are a property of the training loss, available to any reranker fine-tune.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
No reranker fine-tune of the 27B backbone exists yet. The fine-tunes saw different training data, which the
zero-shot subset only partly controls; contamination cannot be ruled out. Literature numbers are not recomputed. Calibration is measured on TREC DL only; other uses of calibrated
probabilities, such as query performance prediction, are left to future work.
