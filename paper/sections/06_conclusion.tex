\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

On the same backbone, reranker fine-tuning beats decision fine-tuning wherever both exist (RQ1), and decision models
are not better calibrated than reranker fine-tunes (RQ2): the probabilities that set them apart come from the
training loss and are available to any reranker fine-tune. \emph{Limitations}: no 27B reranker fine-tune exists
yet; the fine-tunes saw different training data, which the zero-shot subset only partly controls; literature numbers
are not recomputed or tested; calibration is measured on TREC DL only.
