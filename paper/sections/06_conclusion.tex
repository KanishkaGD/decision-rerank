\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Do System One models make better rerankers? No. On the same foundation model, reranker fine-tuning beats decision
fine-tuning, which adds little to the untuned model. Their calibration is no advantage either: reranker fine-tunes
are as well calibrated, select better passages at a fixed threshold, and can be trained to be better calibrated than
any decision model. Despite the hype and first uses as rerankers, decision models are currently of no use for
reranking, neither for ranking nor for their calibration.
