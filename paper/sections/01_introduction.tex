\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

System One models, also called decision models, answer a question about an input (the \emph{state}) with a
probability instead of generated text, like fast, intuitive thinking~\cite{Kahneman2011}. TypeSafe AI
released the first of them, \jev{}, on 15 September 2026, trained with a new method it calls RLCD (reinforcement
learning for calibrated decisions)~\cite{TypeSafe2026Jev}. The model class is only about a month old, yet it is
marketed widely, and first reports show \jev{} reranking BM25 candidates about as well as strong
cross-encoders~\cite{S1Rank,JevRerank,LanceDB2026,Elastic2026}. Open alternatives with the same kind of output
followed within weeks: Kev by the end of September~\cite{Kev} and Cloudflare's Clef on 1 October~\cite{Clef}. They
fine-tune open Qwen models, but not with RLCD, which is unpublished~\cite{KevCards}. Still, the largest Kev matches \jev{} as a reranker in our experiments. We therefore study
\emph{decision fine-tuning}, i.e.\ fine-tuning an open model to give \jev{}'s kind of output, and ask, one month
after \jev{}'s release, whether it is of use for retrieval.

A fair test compares decision fine-tuning with the established alternative, reranker fine-tuning, on the same
\emph{foundation model}, i.e.\ the same pretrained language model of a given size, such as Qwen3.5-4B. For six Qwen
foundation models from 0.6B to 27B parameters and ten test collections, we compare three variants: the base model
asked zero-shot, its decision fine-tune and its reranker fine-tune. Since decision models are advertised for their
calibrated probabilities, we also check whether they at least deliver that:

Both answers are mostly negative. (1) Wherever both exist (0.6B to 9B), the reranker fine-tune ranks better than
the decision fine-tune, also on collections without training data, and up to 9B decision fine-tuning hardly
improves on the base model. (2) Decision models are better calibrated than reranker fine-tunes of the same model only
if the question matches the relevance definition, so their calibration depends on the wording. Even then they
select worse passages at a fixed threshold, and the untuned 27B model counts relevant passages best. Our
own reranker fine-tune of Qwen3.5-9B is as well calibrated as the best decision model of its size and ranks as well
as the best published reranker. Code, raw outputs and the results on all collections are available.\footnote{\TODO{anonymised link}}
