  \caption{What we ask. Each Qwen backbone is compared in three variants that all return a probability of relevance
  for a query and a BM25 candidate: the base model asked zero-shot, its decision fine-tunes (System One models such as
  Kev and Clef) and its reranker fine-tunes. \RQ{1} asks which variant ranks best, \RQ{2} whether the decision models
  are at least better calibrated and whether that is of any use. An ablation asks how much both answers depend on the
  wording of the question put to the model.}
  \Description{A diagram. On the left, one Qwen backbone branches into three variants: a zero-shot base model, a
  decision fine-tune and a reranker fine-tune; all three read a query with its BM25 top-100 candidates and output a
  probability. On the right, three boxes: RQ1 about ranking (nDCG@10), RQ2 about calibration and its uses (ECE, a fixed
  cutoff, counting relevant passages), and the question ablation contrasting two wordings of the relevance question.}
