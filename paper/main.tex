  \input{figures/teaser}
  \caption{How System One models rerank and what we compare. (a)~A decision model is asked, for each BM25
  candidate, whether the passage answers the query, and the candidates are sorted by the probability of ``yes''. The
  state, the input the model is asked about, holds one passage (isolated mode) or all 100 candidates (joint mode). (b)~On one Qwen backbone, the untuned
  model, its decision fine-tunes and its reranker fine-tunes rerank the same candidates and are compared on ranking
  and on calibration.}
  \Description{Two panels. Panel a: a query with one passage per state (isolated mode) or with all 100 candidate
  passages in one state (joint mode) goes into a decision model together with the question ``Does document doc answer
  the query?'', asked once per passage. The model returns a probability of yes for every passage, shown as bars on
  example passages in BM25 order, and sorting by this probability gives the reranked list. Panel b: one Qwen backbone
  of 0.6B to 27B parameters branches into three variants: the untuned model asked zero-shot, a decision fine-tune (Kev,
  Clef) and a reranker fine-tune (Qwen3-Reranker, Prism, ours). All three read the same BM25 top-100 of TREC DL
  2019--2023 and five BEIR collections and return a probability of yes, which is evaluated for ranking (nDCG@10) and
  for calibration (predicted probability against observed relevance).}
  \label{fig:teaser}
\end{teaserfigure}
