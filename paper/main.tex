\begin{abstract}
System One models, or decision models, answer a question about an input with a calibrated probability. They are
only a month old but much discussed, and first reports have tried them as rerankers. For six Qwen foundation models
(0.6B to 27B parameters) and ten test collections, we compare the untuned model with its decision fine-tunes and its
reranker fine-tunes, one of which we trained ourselves. The reranker fine-tunes rank better wherever both exist,
while decision fine-tuning adds little to the untuned model. Decision models are better calibrated only if the
question matches the relevance definition, and even then reranker fine-tunes select better passages at a fixed
threshold. Our own reranker fine-tune is as well calibrated as the best decision model of its size.
\keywords{Reranking \and Calibration \and System One Models \and Fine-Tuning}
\end{abstract}
