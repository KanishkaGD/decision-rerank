\begin{abstract}
System One models, or decision models, answer a question about an input with a calibrated probability. They are
only a month old but much discussed, and first reports have tried them as rerankers. For six Qwen models (0.6B to
27B parameters) and ten test collections, we compare the untuned model with its decision fine-tunes and its reranker
fine-tunes, one of which we trained ourselves. The reranker fine-tunes rank better wherever both exist, while
decision fine-tuning adds little to the untuned model. Decision models are not better calibrated either: with a
fixed probability threshold, reranker fine-tunes select better passages, and our own reranker fine-tune gives the
most reliable probabilities of all systems we tested.
\keywords{Reranking \and Calibration \and System One Models \and Fine-Tuning}
\end{abstract}
